Lawn Care on Hilton Head Island

Why Hilton Head Lawns Need a Different Approach

Hilton Head Island is one of the most beautiful places on the East Coast — and one of the most demanding environments for lawn care. The combination of salt air from ocean and tidal exposure, sandy and often nutrient-poor soil, a short window in which to apply the right products at the right time, and high aesthetic expectations from HOA communities all make Hilton Head lawn care more complex than most national programs acknowledge.

InfiniteAg works with Hilton Head homeowners to assess what's actually happening in their specific yard before making any recommendation. Whether your lawn is showing stress from salt exposure, drought, poor soil nutrition, fungal disease, or pest damage — the first step is accurate diagnosis.

  • Coastal soil and turf health assessment
  • Salt damage assessment and mitigation guidance
  • Brown patch and disease diagnosis
  • Weed identification and management
  • Seasonal turf care for warm-season grasses
  • Edible garden and landscape design

Salt Air & Exposure

Properties near the ocean and tidal marshes face salt stress on turf, ornamentals, and edible plants. Salt-tolerant plant selection and proper leaching irrigation help manage this.

Sandy Soil

Hilton Head's sandy soil drains fast and holds few nutrients. Without consistent organic matter input and targeted fertilization, lawns thin out quickly.

HOA Lawn Standards

Many Hilton Head communities have specific appearance standards. InfiniteAg helps maintain quality turf that meets HOA expectations without unnecessary chemical use.

Deer & Wildlife Pressure

Deer pressure on edible plants and ornamentals is significant on Hilton Head. We plan edible gardens with realistic expectations around wildlife access.

InfiniteAg Services Available in Hilton Head

The same plant scientist-led approach that serves Bluffton homeowners extends to Hilton Head Island and the surrounding Lowcountry.

Lawn Care & Turf Health

Regular mowing, edging, seasonal turf inspections, weed management, and soil-informed fertilization for Hilton Head's warm-season lawns. Zoysia, St. Augustine, and Bermuda are all common on the island and each needs slightly different care.

Soil Health Assessment

Hilton Head's sandy, salt-influenced soil often has specific nutrient gaps and drainage characteristics. We assess before recommending any amendment program so you're addressing what's actually missing — not guessing.

Yard Diagnosis

Brown patches, fungal disease, insect damage, and drainage problems are all common on Hilton Head. InfiniteAg identifies what's causing the problem so you don't spend money treating the wrong thing.

Edible Landscape Design

Hilton Head's warm climate and long growing season are well-suited to productive edible landscapes. We design with the island's salt exposure, deer pressure, and HOA considerations in mind.

Raised Garden Bed Consultation

Raised beds are especially valuable on Hilton Head where native soil is often sandy and low in organic matter. We advise on placement, soil mix, and crop selection for the island's coastal growing conditions.

Sustainable Landscape Planning

Reduced chemical use, pollinator plantings, native species integration, and water-efficient landscaping — all of particular value in Hilton Head's ecologically sensitive coastal environment.
