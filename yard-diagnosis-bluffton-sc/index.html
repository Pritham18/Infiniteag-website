Yard Problem Diagnosis

Common Yard Problems InfiniteAg Diagnoses in Bluffton

If your yard has one of these issues — or something else that's been puzzling you — a plant scientist-led diagnosis is the right first step.

Brown Patches & Dead Spots

Brown patches in Bluffton lawns are commonly caused by Large Patch fungus, irrigation failure, insect damage, or soil compaction. Without diagnosis, treating the wrong cause wastes time and money. We identify which one — or which combination — is affecting your lawn.

Bare or Thin Patches

Thinning turf in the Lowcountry is often a sign of soil problems, shading changes, improper mowing height, or competition from weeds and disease. Overseeding bare spots without addressing the cause produces poor results. We find out why the grass isn't growing before recommending how to fix it.

Weeds Taking Over

Persistent weeds — nutsedge, crabgrass, dollarweed, chamberbitter — are symptoms of stressed turf and poor soil conditions. They thrive where grass is struggling. Our diagnosis identifies what's limiting your lawn's density so weeds can't take hold.

Drainage Problems & Standing Water

Pooling water in Bluffton yards is most often caused by clay soil layers, grade issues, compaction, or irrigation over-application. Standing water damages roots, fuels fungal disease, and creates mosquito problems. We evaluate where the drainage breakdown is happening before recommending a fix.

Poor or Failing Plant Growth

New plants that fail to establish, shrubs that decline after installation, or established trees and perennials that drop foliage unexpectedly — these often come back to soil conditions, drainage, or root zone problems that aren't visible at the surface.

Yellowing Leaves & Nutrient Signs

Yellow or discolored lawn and garden plants can indicate nutrient deficiency, pH imbalance, over-irrigation, or disease. The visual symptom looks similar across multiple causes — soil testing and plant assessment reveal which one applies to your Bluffton property.

How It Works

What Happens During a Yard Diagnosis

When InfiniteAg comes to your Bluffton property, we don't just look at the symptom — we look at the whole yard. We evaluate turf condition, soil texture and compaction, drainage patterns, irrigation coverage, sun exposure, and any visible disease or pest signs.

Then we walk you through what we found. You'll understand what's causing the problem, why it's happening, and what your realistic options are to fix it — including any tradeoffs between approaches. You don't need to take notes. We'll follow up with clear written recommendations.

Depending on the scope of the problem, we can provide a one-time diagnostic report, a treatment plan you implement yourself, or an ongoing service plan with InfiniteAg.

Questions

Yard Diagnosis — FAQ

Can you diagnose yard problems remotely or via photos?

Photos can help us understand what you're seeing before a visit, but a reliable diagnosis requires seeing the yard in person — especially for soil, drainage, and root zone issues that don't show up in photos. InfiniteAg provides on-site assessments for Bluffton, Hilton Head, Beaufort, and other Lowcountry locations.

What if the yard problem turns out to be something simple?

Sometimes it is — and that's fine. You'll leave with a clear answer and actionable next steps, whether simple or complex. No unnecessary upselling. If the fix is straightforward, we'll tell you what to do yourself.

Can you diagnose problems with edible gardens or raised beds too?

Yes. InfiniteAg diagnoses problems in vegetable gardens, raised beds, edible landscapes, and ornamental plantings — not just lawns. Soil quality, drainage, watering, and nutrient availability affect edible plants just as much as turf.
