What We Offer

Software, Apps & Growing Products

From growing management tools to the plants and biological controls that go in the ground, InfiniteAg's products support every stage of a productive, sustainable growing system.

Book Consultation Browse Plants
Software

Growing Management Software

Web-based tools for tracking soil data, planning crops, and keeping a running record of what's happening across your property, garden, or growing operation.

Soil & Crop Data Dashboard

A central record of soil test results, assessments, and recommendations for your property, so nothing is lost between visits.

Crop & Season Planning

Plan what goes where and when, based on your soil data and the varieties best suited to your site.

Apps

Apps That Keep Your Growing Plan on Track

Mobile tools that help you monitor soil, irrigation, and plant health between visits — so your yard, garden, or plot stays on track year-round.

Soil & Moisture Monitoring

Paired with in-ground sensors, track real-time soil moisture and temperature and get alerted before conditions get too dry or too wet.

Smart Irrigation Scheduling

Weather-linked irrigation controllers adjust watering automatically based on rainfall and temperature.

Planting & Harvest Tracking

Digital planting calendars and crop-tracking tools log what's in the ground and expected harvest windows.

Plant & Pest Identification

Camera-based identification tools help flag disease or pest pressure early, before it spreads.

Property & Garden Mapping

Simple digital mapping of beds, irrigation zones, and planting areas to track what's changed season over season.

Client Reporting

Soil results and recommendations shared digitally, so you always have a record of what was found and why.

InfiniteAg helps select and set up the right combination of tools for your property size and goals — recommended based on what you actually need, not a one-size-fits-all bundle.
