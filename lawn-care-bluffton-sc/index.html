Brown Patch Diagnosis

Fungal disease, insect damage, irrigation failure, or soil compaction can all produce the same brown look. InfiniteAg identifies the actual cause before recommending any treatment — so you're not spending money on the wrong fix.

Weed Pressure

Nutsedge, crabgrass, and dollarweed thrive where turf is thin or stressed. Pulling weeds without addressing the underlying soil or irrigation issue means they return. We identify why your lawn is losing ground to weeds.

Soil & Root Health

Bluffton's sandy and sometimes compacted coastal soils need specific amendments — not a generic fertilizer schedule. InfiniteAg assesses your soil's actual condition before recommending any inputs.

Seasonal Lawn Timing

Warm-season grasses in SC have narrow windows for fertilization, pre-emergent applications, and weed control. Applying products at the wrong time can damage turf or waste money on treatments that don't take effect.

Lowcountry Lawn Challenges

What Makes Bluffton Lawns Different

South Carolina's Lowcountry has a unique climate and soil profile. Understanding it is the first step to better lawn care.

Warm-Season Grasses

St. Augustine, Zoysia, Centipede, and Bermuda are the dominant grasses in Bluffton. Each one has different fertilizer, mowing, and water requirements — and most lawn companies treat them all the same way.

Summer Heat & Humidity

Bluffton's hot, humid summers create ideal conditions for fungal diseases like Large Patch and Brown Patch. Proper watering timing and air circulation matter more than many homeowners realize.

Sandy & Coastal Soil

Much of the Lowcountry has sandy, low-nutrient soil that drains too quickly or compacted fill soil from development. Both conditions require specific amendments — and knowing which one you have matters.

Fall Lawn Care Timing

Applying the wrong products at the wrong time in fall — when warm-season grasses go dormant — can damage your lawn. Fall is also the highest-risk time for Large Patch disease in Bluffton.

Drainage Variation

Properties in Bluffton range from well-draining sandy soil to low-lying areas with standing water after rain. Drainage directly affects turf health, root depth, and disease pressure.

Why Science Matters Here

Generic lawn programs ignore these local variables. InfiniteAg's plant science approach means your lawn gets care based on what's actually happening in your specific yard — not a formula built for a national average.

Lawn Care in Bluffton — FAQ

Do you mow lawns in Bluffton, SC?

Yes. Mowing and edging are part of our Essential Lawn Care package for Bluffton homeowners. We also provide mowing guidance as part of our assessment process — including the right cutting height for your grass type, which has a significant impact on turf health in the Lowcountry.

How do I know if my Bluffton lawn has a soil problem?

Signs include thinning or patchy grass that doesn't respond to watering, compacted soil that doesn't absorb rain, persistent weeds that return after removal, and slow or poor growth even with regular fertilizer. A soil health assessment is the most reliable way to find out what's actually limiting your lawn's health.

What warm-season grasses do you work with in Bluffton?

InfiniteAg works with all warm-season grasses common to Bluffton and the SC Lowcountry, including St. Augustine, Zoysia, Bermuda, and Centipede. Each grass type has different requirements for fertilization, mowing height, watering, and seasonal care — we tailor recommendations accordingly.
