Lawn & Garden Care for Okatie's Growing Communities

Okatie has grown rapidly over the past decade, with developments like Bayside, Lawton Station, and the communities surrounding Sun City Hilton Head bringing thousands of new homes to the area. Many of these properties share a common challenge: lawns established on builder fill, stripped topsoil, or sandy substrate with limited organic matter and poor structure for long-term turf health.

Newer construction lawns in Okatie often look adequate at first but struggle as the builder soil compacts, nutrients deplete, and the grass's root system can't establish properly. Weeds move in, brown patches appear, and watering and fertilizing more doesn't fix the underlying problem.

My new Okatie lawn looks patchy after a year — what's happening?

Patchy lawns after the first year or two in newer developments almost always trace back to the soil condition under the sod. Builder fill with limited topsoil depth, compaction from construction equipment, and missing organic matter cause root systems to stay shallow and turf to thin as initial nutrients run out. A soil assessment is the right first step to understand what's actually limiting your lawn.
