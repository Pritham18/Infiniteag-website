Our Mission

Helping People Grow Smarter, Healthier Food Systems

InfiniteAg helps homes, businesses, and communities turn indoor and outdoor spaces into productive, sustainable growing systems — grounded in real plant science, not guesswork.

Dr. Bazgha Zia Plant Scientist
The Science Behind InfiniteAg

Dr. Bazgha Zia

Plant Scientist

Dr. Bazgha Zia brings applied plant science and soil diagnostics to homeowners and small growers — the kind of data-driven decision-making typically reserved for commercial agriculture, scaled to fit a residential lawn, a raised bed, an indoor farm, or a small working farm.

What sets InfiniteAg apart is simple: every recommendation is grounded in measured soil and plant data, not a generic service schedule. That means treatments, crop choices, and growing systems are calibrated to what's actually happening on your property.

  • Soil, turf, and growing-condition diagnostics
  • Indoor farming & small farm growing-system consulting
  • Edible landscape and food-production design
  • Sustainable, data-informed land management
