Common Yard & Lawn Problems

Problems InfiniteAg Helps Solve

If any of these sound familiar, a plant scientist-led assessment is the right first step. We diagnose before we recommend — serving Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Summerville, and the SC Lowcountry.

Brown Patches & Weak Grass

Brown spots are a symptom, not the root cause. Soil compaction, poor drainage, fungal disease, heat stress, or nutrient imbalance can all look identical at the surface. InfiniteAg identifies which factor is actually at work before recommending any fix.

Weeds Taking Over

Persistent weeds like nutsedge, crabgrass, and dollarweed thrive in stressed or thin turf. Treating weeds alone — without addressing underlying soil or grass health — creates a cycle that returns every season. InfiniteAg starts with why weeds are winning.

Poor Drainage & Soggy Areas

Pooling water after rain, persistently muddy spots, or turf that won't dry out usually points to compaction, a clay layer below the surface, or a grade issue. We evaluate where the drainage breakdown is actually happening before recommending any solution.

Compacted or Depleted Soil

Soil that won't absorb water, won't hold nutrients, or produces shallow roots needs structural improvement — not just more fertilizer. InfiniteAg identifies what's limiting soil health and recommends amendments specific to your property's actual conditions.

Struggling Vegetable Garden

Plants that fail to fruit, seedlings that die off, or vegetables that grow slowly despite watering almost always trace back to soil quality, drainage, or sunlight issues. We diagnose before recommending what to change — saving time on trial and error.

Unsure What to Plant Here

Not every plant that grows elsewhere in the U.S. thrives in Charleston's summer heat and humidity. InfiniteAg helps Lowcountry homeowners select the right grass, edible plants, and landscape species for their specific site conditions and goals.

Bare or Thin Patches

Bare spots that don't recover after reseeding usually mean conditions in that area — compaction, shade, drainage, or the wrong grass type — aren't supporting establishment. Adding seed without addressing the cause produces limited results.

Want Edibles, Not Just Ornamentals

Many SC homeowners want a yard that actually produces something — herbs near the kitchen, fruit trees in the beds, vegetables in raised planters. InfiniteAg designs edible landscapes that fit your space, soil, and available time to maintain.
