Common Problems, Measured — Not Guessed

Diagnosable Problems Across Lawns, Gardens & Small Farms

If any of these sound familiar, a plant scientist-led assessment is the right first step. We diagnose before we recommend — serving homeowners and small farms across Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Summerville, and the SC Lowcountry.

Brown Patches & Weak Turf

Compaction, drainage, disease, heat stress, and nutrient imbalance all look similar at the surface. Soil-level data isolates the actual cause.

Persistent Weeds

Weeds exploit stressed, thin turf. Treating weeds without correcting the underlying soil condition creates a recurring cycle — we identify why weeds are winning first.

Drainage & Compaction Issues

Pooling water and compacted soil point to a structural problem below ground. We measure where the breakdown is actually occurring.

Compacted or Depleted Soil

Soil that won't absorb water, won't hold nutrients, or produces shallow roots needs structural improvement — not just more fertilizer. InfiniteAg identifies what's limiting soil health and recommends amendments specific to your property's actual conditions.

Underperforming Vegetable Gardens or Small Plots

Poor fruiting, slow growth, and seedling failure almost always trace back to soil quality, drainage, or light — diagnosable with the right data, not trial and error.

Uncertain Crop or Plant Selection

Not every crop or turf variety performs in Charleston's heat and humidity. Site-specific data narrows down what will actually succeed on your property.

Small Farms Without an Agronomist

Many small farms and homesteads operate without access to soil mapping or crop-planning support. InfiniteAg fills that gap with scaled-down precision-ag consulting.

Tools That Keep Working After We Leave

Apps & Digital Tools to Extend Your Growing Plan

A property scan and a plan are the starting point. InfiniteAg also connects homeowners and small growers with the digital tools that keep soil, irrigation, and planting decisions on track between visits — so your yard, garden, or plot stays productive year-round, not just after a service call.

Soil & Moisture Monitoring Apps

Paired with in-ground sensors, these apps track real-time soil moisture and temperature and alert you before a lawn, bed, or crop area gets too dry or stays too wet — replacing guesswork watering with actual data.

Smart Irrigation Scheduling

Weather-linked irrigation controllers adjust watering automatically based on rainfall, temperature, and evapotranspiration — cutting water waste and keeping soil conditions consistent across the season.

Planting & Harvest Tracking Tools

Digital planting calendars and crop-tracking apps log what's in the ground, expected harvest windows, and rotation history — useful for edible landscapes and essential for small farm and homestead planning.

Plant & Pest Identification Apps

Camera-based identification tools help flag disease, pest pressure, or nutrient deficiency early, so issues get addressed before they spread — a helpful second set of eyes between InfiniteAg visits.

Property & Garden Mapping

Simple digital mapping of beds, irrigation zones, and planting areas keeps a running record of your property — useful for tracking what's been amended, planted, or changed season over season.

Client Reporting & Recommendations

Soil test results, assessment findings, and care recommendations are shared digitally, so you always have a record of what was measured, what was recommended, and why — not just a verbal summary after a visit.

InfiniteAg helps select and set up the right combination of tools for your property size and goals — from a simple moisture sensor for a raised bed to a full irrigation and monitoring setup for a small farm plot. We recommend tools based on what your property actually needs, not a one-size-fits-all app bundle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about InfiniteAg's services, service areas, and what to expect during an assessment.

What areas does InfiniteAg serve?

Charleston, Mount Pleasant, and Summerville, SC, plus nearby Lowcountry areas including Bluffton, Hilton Head Island, Beaufort, Okatie, and Sun City Hilton Head. Call or text (803) 903-7059 to confirm your area.

What makes InfiniteAg different from a standard lawn or farm service?

InfiniteAg is led by a plant scientist and built around soil and plant data rather than fixed schedules. We measure soil health, isolate root-cause problems, and calibrate every recommendation to your property — whether that's a residential yard or a small working farm. We also offer small farm consulting, edible system design, and indoor/container growing guidance most lawn companies don't touch.

Do you work with small farms, not just residential lawns?

Yes. InfiniteAg provides soil mapping, crop planning, and growing-system consulting scaled for small acreage and homestead operations — bringing the diagnostic approach of larger agricultural operations down to a size that fits small growers.

Can you diagnose brown patches, weeds, or crop underperformance?

Yes. Diagnosis is core to what we do. These issues all have measurable underlying causes — soil condition, drainage, nutrient gaps — and we identify the actual driver before recommending treatment.

Do you design food-producing systems?

Yes. We design edible landscapes and small-farm growing systems around your soil data, sunlight, water access, and maintenance capacity.

Can you help with raised beds or small-plot growing?

Yes. Our system design covers placement, soil mix, crop sequencing, irrigation, and seasonal planting — built for repeatable, measurable output.

Can you improve poor, sandy, or depleted soil?

Yes. Soil diagnostics are the foundation of every InfiniteAg service. We assess compaction, drainage, nutrients, and biology, then recommend targeted amendments — not a generic fertilizer program.

Do you use or recommend apps and technology for my yard or farm?

Yes. Alongside our on-site diagnostics, InfiniteAg helps set up digital tools — soil moisture sensors, smart irrigation scheduling, planting and harvest tracking apps, and plant/pest identification tools — matched to your property size and goals, so you can stay on top of watering, planting, and problem spotting between visits.
