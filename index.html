How It Works

No sales pressure, no complicated contracts — just a clear, data-backed path from first visit to harvest.

  1. 01

    Discover Your Space

    Tell us about your indoor space, yard, garden, or plot — and your growing goals.

  2. 02

    Diagnose Soil / Growing Conditions

    Soil, drainage, sunlight, and growing conditions are measured directly, not estimated.

  3. 03

    Select the Best Varieties

    Plants and crop varieties are matched to what your space and soil can actually support.

  4. 04

    Build Your Growing System

    Layout, soil, irrigation, and planting come together into a working growing system.

  5. 05

    Monitor, Improve, and Harvest

    Ongoing support or a one-time plan — either way, you have a system built to produce.
